# Constraints

Constraints limit what entities and processes can do. They make knowledge and capability meaningful.

## Candidate constraint families

- Physical laws and environmental conditions
- Limited matter, energy, time, computation, and bandwidth
- Sensor range, resolution, noise, and uncertainty
- Manufacturing precision and throughput
- Travel distance, velocity, acceleration, and risk
- Biological survival requirements
- Communication delay and information loss
- Conflicting human objectives and institutional behavior
- Maintenance, degradation, and failure
- Incomplete or incorrect models

## Design rule

Constraints should create understandable tradeoffs and opportunities for invention. Avoid arbitrary locks that exist only to delay the User.
