# Resources

Resources are finite, transferable, consumable, renewable, or informational inputs used by processes.

## Candidate categories

- Matter and raw materials
- Energy and power
- Propellant
- Computation
- Storage
- Time
- Manufacturing capacity
- Communication bandwidth
- Human labor and attention
- Trust, legitimacy, and cooperation
- Knowledge and validated models

## Design caution

Knowledge may behave like a resource in gameplay, but truth is not consumed when used. Distinguish access, confidence, storage, transmission, and application from physical consumption.

Detailed resource units and balances are not yet defined.
