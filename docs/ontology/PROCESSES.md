# Processes

Processes are verbs that change world state.

## Candidate processes

- Observe and scan
- Communicate
- Travel and navigate
- Acquire and transport resources
- Manufacture and assemble
- Repair and maintain
- Store and retrieve information
- Research and model
- Replicate
- Negotiate, coordinate, command, or coerce
- Sustain living populations
- Colonize and expand

## Modeling rule

A process should specify:

1. participants;
2. required capabilities;
3. consumed resources;
4. environmental constraints;
5. produced state changes;
6. observable evidence;
7. failure modes.

No process beyond launching and greeting the User is implemented in the repository baseline.
