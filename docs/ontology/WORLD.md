# World

This document defines the universe as a system, not as lore.

## Initial scope

The world contains natural bodies, environments, living populations, constructed systems, resources, information, and processes that continue whether or not the User observes them.

## Seed statements

- A **star system** contains stars, natural bodies, resources, environments, and constructed entities.
- A **natural body** may have gravity, atmosphere, temperature, radiation, terrain, and accessible resources.
- An **environment** can enable, degrade, or prevent processes and survival.
- **Humanity** consists of individuals, populations, institutions, settlements, knowledge, needs, and competing objectives.
- The **User** acts through available bodies, systems, sensors, models, tools, and relationships.
- The state of the world is separate from what the User knows about it.

## Boundary

Detailed star systems, political factions, alien species, and historical lore are not yet defined. Add them only when a capability or player experience requires them.
