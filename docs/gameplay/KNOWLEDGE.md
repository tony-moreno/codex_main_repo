# Knowledge

The universe's state and the User's knowledge of that state are separate.

## Layers

1. **Truth:** What is actually true in the simulation.
2. **Evidence:** What observations or reports are available.
3. **Interpretation:** What the User or another actor believes the evidence means.
4. **Model:** A structured explanation that can predict outcomes.
5. **Validated knowledge:** A model supported strongly enough to enable reliable action.
6. **Design knowledge:** Understanding that can be composed into tools, systems, or procedures.

## Principles

- Discovery changes the User's model, not the underlying world.
- Observations may be incomplete, noisy, delayed, misleading, or misinterpreted.
- Research should produce new explanatory or predictive power, not only spend points.
- Knowledge should unlock better questions as well as better actions.
- Multiple paths may produce equivalent understanding.
- Previously learned principles should combine into capabilities the designers did not enumerate explicitly.

No research mechanics are implemented in the baseline repository.
