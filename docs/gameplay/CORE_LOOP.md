# Core Loop

The intended mature loop is:

1. **Observe** the world through available sensors, reports, and consequences.
2. **Question** what is happening and what remains unknown.
3. **Experiment** within physical, informational, and social constraints.
4. **Learn** by converting evidence into increasingly reliable models.
5. **Design** a new use of known capabilities or a path toward a missing capability.
6. **Construct** or configure something that can act in the world.
7. **Deploy** it and accept cost, uncertainty, and risk.
8. **Experience consequences** as the world and its inhabitants respond.
9. **Repeat** with a larger—but never complete—understanding.
