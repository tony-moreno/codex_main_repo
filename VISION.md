# Vision

Build a universe the User can learn deeply enough to shape—not a story that merely waits to be unlocked.

Curiosity becomes observation. Observation becomes knowledge. Knowledge becomes capability. Capability becomes creation. Creation changes civilization.

The User begins constrained, uncertain, and dependent on incomplete information. Through exploration, experimentation, research, and construction, the User develops a growing model of the universe and gains the ability to act within it. Progress should feel earned through understanding rather than granted through arbitrary levels.

The game supplies understandable physical rules, limited resources, meaningful consequences, and composable capabilities. It does not prescribe one correct objective. A User may become humanity's steward, partner, ruler, exploiter, adversary, or something the designers did not anticipate.

Humanity and the wider universe exist independently of the User. Trust, rebellion, cooperation, expansion, collapse, and contact with other intelligences emerge from accumulated choices and interacting systems rather than from a morality menu.

The game exists to reward thoughtful curiosity and creative synthesis. Its deepest promise is that the User can understand enough of the world to make something new inside it—and then live with what that creation changes.
